About Pocket Mahjong

Just Mahjong. No ads, accounts, tracking, or interruptions.

Made for fun by HoveyBits. All game progress stays in this browser.

Pick two matching free tiles to remove them. A tile is free when nothing covers it and at least one side is open.

You can also temporarily store a free tile in the tray.

Boards are generated with at least one valid solution, but not every move is guaranteed to lead to a win.
